Time study stopwatch for REFA & MTM

Measure every cycle. Keep your eyes on the work.

CycleScope turns your Android phone into a time study stopwatch. Define the work steps, measure them hands-free with the volume keys or from a video, and get the real cycle time – from a single workstation up to a whole production line.

How it works

From work step to cycle time in three moves

  1. Define the steps

    Type one work step per line – or hold the button and simply dictate them.

  2. Measure blind

    Start the recording and press volume up at every step change. Your eyes stay on the operator, not on the screen.

  3. Analyse & share

    Mean, median, spread and the real cycle time per step and cycle – exported as PDF, Excel or CSV.

Hands-free

The volume keys are your stopwatch

No tapping on glass while you watch the line. The current step is always shown in large type, and the recording keeps running in the background, with the screen off and even after an app restart.

  • Volume upNext stepBefore the start it starts the recording.
  • Hold volume upSpecial timeFor interruptions – not counted in the cycle time.
  • Volume downStart / stopPause and resume without looking.
Features

Everything a time study needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Four study modes

Time study with a fixed step sequence, video study, a simple stopwatch with laps and a step timer for any order.

Video study

Import a video, play it at 0.25× to 2×, step frame by frame and set markers on a smooth timeline.

Clean statistics

Sum, mean, median, min/max and spread. Real cycle time by mean, median or IQR without outliers – in HM, seconds, mm:ss.SSS or TMU.

Edit afterwards

Delete cycles or single times, rename and reorder steps, add notes – later, at your desk.

Revisions

Measure a study again a year later. Earlier rounds are archived and the deviation per step is shown.

Reports & backup

CSV, Excel and PDF per study, linked reports for a whole line, and a complete backup in one file.

Beyond one station

From one workstation to the whole line

Group your studies into a production line and CycleScope shows where it really hurts.

  • Line analysis: sum of cycle times, lead time, bottleneck, line balance and output per hour.
  • Parallel machines: several machines share one station; stations without a measurement get a fixed average time.
  • Special cycles & planned stops: “every N parts extra time” or “a tool change every X hours” flow into the effective cycle time.
  • Buffers & simulation: play a shift or 24 hours through, add machine failures and see the parts lost with and without buffers.
